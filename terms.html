06Tuner readings, calculations and safety

In short: TuneForge is designed to be accurate, but it’s a guide. Check before adjusting your instrument, and take care with string tension and volume.

We work hard to make TuneForge accurate and reliable. Its readings and results also depend on things outside our control, so they’re provided as a guide:

  • Pitch detection depends on your device’s microphone, background noise, the sound of your instrument and how it’s played.
  • Fret positions are calculated from the scale length you enter, using standard fret-spacing formulas. Real instruments also depend on nut and saddle placement, compensation and build tolerances.
  • String tension figures are estimates for plain steel strings, based on the values you enter. Manufacturers’ published figures and real-world conditions may differ.
  • Chord shapes and transpositions are standard references. You may prefer other voicings, and some shapes won’t suit every instrument or tuning.

Before cutting, filing, fretting or otherwise altering your instrument, or tuning to a higher tension than usual, please check the manufacturer’s guidance or ask a qualified technician. Bringing strings up to pitch too quickly or too high can damage an instrument or make a string break, so tune gradually and keep your face and eyes clear of the strings.

When you use the metronome, the drone or reference notes, keep the volume at a comfortable level, especially with headphones. Prolonged listening at high volume can damage your hearing.

To the extent the law allows, we’re not responsible for damage to instruments or equipment that results from relying on a reading or calculation without taking these precautions. This doesn’t limit our responsibility for defective digital content described in section 12.
